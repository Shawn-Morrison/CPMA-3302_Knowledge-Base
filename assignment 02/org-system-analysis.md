# Analyzing an Organizing System:

I have chosen [PokemonROM]([Pokemon ROMs Download - Pokemon Rom](https://pokemonrom.net/)) as my Organizing system for this assignment. I discovered this digital resource today when a company advertised for a phone case that was designed to help simulate playing a Game Boy by covering your mobile phone with a case that resembled one.

This online website is a digital collection of Pokémon game files for download for use in emulators. The files will allow you to play classic games of your past without having to purchase entire systems or games. The site's organization is designed to help users efficiently discover and locate the content that best matches their interests.

## Primary resources:

The primary resources being organized are game files. The game files are sorted and organized into different categories. The categories are listed by system name: GBA (Game Boy Advanced), 3DS, GameCube, NDS, N64, GBC (Game Boy Color), and a few non specific categories listed as All ROMs, Global ROMs, ROM Hack, and Fan Games.

### Primary interactions:

- **Browsing:** You can **Browse** by looking at *trending ROMs* to see what is the most popular, or by searching for your favorite game. 
- **Searching:** You can also **Search** each category to see what games are listed by category. 
- **Downloading:** You can **Download** ROM files to your emulator storage to be able to play games.


### Classification system:

This organizing system utilizes the following classification system:

- Hierarchical: You can navigate through the classifications and narrow down to the ROM you want. They are classified by Game system, so there are not very many levels, which makes it easy to find what you are looking for.

You can search, but there are no filters after searching.

It is very effective for the intended audience. If someone is looking to download a ROM, they are looking for something specific most likely. Most emulator enthusiasts are familiar with different gaming systems and are technically savvy, so they do not need much explanation or detail in their search. There is detail on the game page for each specific game describing the game, providing helpful information about playing the game, an overview of the storyline, and even a walkthrough on most of the games.

### Relationships between resources:

The relationships between resources are pretty poor. There are descriptions of the games in the page where you download the ROM, but there is no link to go back or to another game, except at the very bottom there is a **YOU MAY ALSO LIKE** section, and at the top there is a **HOME** button.
