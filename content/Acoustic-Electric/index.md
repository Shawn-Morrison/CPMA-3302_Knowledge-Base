Acoustic-Electric guitars combine the sound quality and resonance of [[Acoustic/index|acoustic]] guitars, with the ability to be amplified for live performances like [[Electric/index|electic]] guitars. For these reason they are one of the most popular choices of guitars for artists, both beginner and professional.
