# Acoustic-Electric Guitar Maintenance

Proper maintenance keeps [[Acoustic-Electric/index|Acoustic-Electric]] guitars sounding good and playing smoothly for years. Maintenance tasks may depend on [[Acoustic-Electric/construction|construction]], [[Acoustic-Electric/materials|materials]], and the [[Acoustic-Electric/style|style]] of music being played.

## Basic Maintenance Tasks

Regular care helps prevent damage and wear.

- Changing strings regularly
- Cleaning the fretboard
- Wiping down the body after use
- Checking tuning stability
- Testing electronics periodically

> The electronic components of an acoustic-electric guitar require occasional attention. Proper maintenance helps preserve both performance and [[Acoustic-Electric/price|value]].

## Environmental Care

### Temperature and Humidity

- Avoid extreme heat or cold
- Keep humidity stable to prevent wood cracking
- Remove batteries during long-term storage

![[Guitar-Case-Humidifier.jpg|300]]

### Storage Tips

- Use a hard case for long-term storage
- Store with batteries removed if unused for extended periods
- Avoid direct sunlight

## Common Repairs

#### Typical Issues

- String breakage
- Fret wear
- Dead battery
- Loose output jack
- Pickup failure
