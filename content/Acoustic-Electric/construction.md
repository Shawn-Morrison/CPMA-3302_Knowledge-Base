# Acoustic-Electric Guitar Construction

[[Acoustic-Electric/index|Acoustic-Electric]] Guitar construction refers to how the [[Acoustic-Electric/materials|materials]] come together, and the instrument is designed, assembled, and finished. Construction choices can influence the [[Acoustic-Electric/style|style]] of music played, as well as the overall [[Acoustic-Electric/price|price]].

## Main Parts of an Acoustic-Electric Guitar

A standard acoustic-electric guitar is built from several core components:

- Headstock
- Neck
- Fretboard
- Body
- Bridge
- Pickup System
- Preamp

## Specifics for Acoustic-Electric Guitars

- Hollow body design
- Sound hole for resonance
- Internal pickup system
- Built-in preamp and controls
- Output jack for amplification

## Assembly Process

#### Step-by-step Overview

1. Wood selection and cutting
2. Body shaping and routing
3. Neck carving and fret installation
4. Pickup installation
5. Preamp and wiring installation
6. Finishing and painting

> [[Acoustic-Electric/index|Acoustic-Electric]] guitars combine traditional acoustic craftsmanship with modern electronics. The addition of onboard electronics affects both sound and future [[Acoustic-Electric/maintenance|maintenance]] requirements.
