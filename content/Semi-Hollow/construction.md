# Semi-Hollow Guitar Construction

[[Semi-Hollow/index|Semi-Hollow]] Guitar construction refers to how the [[Semi-Hollow/materials|materials]] come together, and the instrument is designed, assembled, and finished. Construction choices can influence the [[Semi-Hollow/style|style]] of music played, as well as the overall [[Semi-Hollow/price|price]].

## Main Parts of a Semi-Hollow Guitar

A standard semi-hollow guitar is built from several core components:

- Headstock
- Neck
- Fretboard
- Body
- Bridge
- Pickups
- Center Block

## Specifics for Semi-Hollow Guitars

- Hollow chambers inside the body
- Solid center block running through the middle
- F-shaped sound holes
- Magnetic pickups
- Output jack for amplification

## Assembly Process

#### Step-by-step Overview

1. Wood selection and cutting
2. Construction of the center block
3. Assembly of hollow body sections
4. Neck carving and fret installation
5. Pickup and electronics installation
6. Finishing and painting

> Semi-hollow guitars combine elements of both acoustic and electric guitar construction. The hollow chambers add warmth and resonance, while the center block improves sustain and reduces unwanted feedback. Construction choices can also affect future [[Semi-Hollow/maintenance|maintenance]] requirements.
