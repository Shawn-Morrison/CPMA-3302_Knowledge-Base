# Classical Guitar Styles of Music

[[Classical/index|Classical]] guitars are known for their warm, mellow tone and expressive fingerstyle techniques. Because of their unique [[Classical/construction|construction]] and traditional [[Classical/materials|materials]], they are commonly associated with several distinctive musical genres.

Common styles include:

- Classical
- Flamenco
- Latin
- Fingerstyle
- Contemporary Instrumental

> Classical guitars emphasize melody, dynamics, and expression. Their nylon strings create a softer sound than steel-string acoustic guitars, making them especially popular for solo performances.

### Popular Examples

- Recuerdos de la Alhambra – **Francisco Tárrega** – *Classical*
- Asturias (Leyenda) – **Isaac Albéniz** – *Classical*
- Entre Dos Aguas – **Paco de Lucía** – *Flamenco*

## Notable Classical Guitar Performance

- Cavatina – **John Williams** – *Classical Guitar*
