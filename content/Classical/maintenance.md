# Classical Guitar Maintenance

Proper maintenance keeps [[Classical/index|Classical]] guitars sounding good and playing smoothly for years. Maintenance tasks may depend on [[Classical/construction|construction]], [[Classical/materials|materials]], and the [[Classical/style|style]] of music being played.

## Basic Maintenance Tasks

Regular care helps prevent damage and wear.

- Replacing nylon strings regularly
- Cleaning the fretboard
- Wiping down the body after use
- Checking tuning stability

> Nylon strings are generally easier on the fingers than steel strings, but they stretch more and require frequent tuning. Proper maintenance helps preserve both performance and [[Classical/price|value]].

## Environmental Care

### Temperature and Humidity

- Avoid extreme heat or cold
- Maintain stable humidity levels
- Protect the soundboard from cracking

![[Guitar-Case-Humidifier.jpg|300]]

### Storage Tips

- Store in a hard case when possible
- Avoid direct sunlight
- Keep away from heating vents

## Common Repairs

#### Typical Issues

- Worn nylon strings
- Loose tuning machines
- Fret wear
- Cracked soundboards
