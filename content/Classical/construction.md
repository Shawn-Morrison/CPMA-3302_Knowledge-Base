# Classical Guitar Construction

[[Classical/index|Classical]] Guitar construction refers to how the [[Classical/materials|materials]] come together, and the instrument is designed, assembled, and finished. Construction choices can influence the [[Classical/style|style]] of music played, as well as the overall [[Classical/price|price]].

## Main Parts of a Classical Guitar

A standard classical guitar is built from several core components:

- Headstock
- Neck
- Fretboard
- Body
- Bridge
- Nylon Strings

## Specifics for Classical Guitars

- Hollow body design
- Wide neck for fingerstyle playing
- Fan bracing beneath the soundboard
- Nylon strings instead of steel strings

## Assembly Process

#### Step-by-step Overview

1. Wood selection and cutting
2. Soundboard bracing installation
3. Body shaping and assembly
4. Neck carving and fret installation
5. Finishing and polishing

> Classical guitar construction has remained largely unchanged for over a century. The traditional design emphasizes resonance, balance, and projection rather than amplification. Construction methods can also influence future [[Classical/maintenance|maintenance]] requirements.
