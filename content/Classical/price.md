# Classical Guitar Pricing

[[Classical/index|Classical]] Guitar prices vary depending on [[Classical/materials|materials]], craftsmanship, and [[Classical/construction|construction]]. Hand-built instruments are particularly valued by professional performers and often command a higher [[Classical/price|price]].

## Price Categories

### Beginner Level ($100–$400)

- Laminated woods
- Student-focused models
- Factory-produced instruments

### Intermediate Level ($400–$1,500)

- Solid tops
- Improved craftsmanship
- Better tonal quality

### Professional Level ($1,500+)

- Handcrafted construction
- Premium tonewoods
- Concert-quality instruments

## Factors That Affect Price

#### Key Influences

- Builder reputation
- Materials used
- Handcrafted construction
- Country of manufacture
- Age and collectability
