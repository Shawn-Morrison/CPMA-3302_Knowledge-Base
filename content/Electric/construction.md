# Electric Guitar Construction

[[Electric/index|Electric]] Guitar construction refers to how the [[Electric/materials|materials]] come together, and the instrument is designed, assembled, and finished. Construction choices can influence the [[Electric/style|style]] of music played, as well as the overall [[Electric/price|price]].

## Main Parts of an Electric Guitar

A standard electric guitar is built from several core components:

- Headstock
- Neck
- Fretboard
- Body
- Bridge
- Pickups

## Specifics for Electric Guitars

- Solid body design
- Magnetic pickups
- Volume and tone controls
- Output jack for amplifiers

## Assembly Process

#### Step-by-step Overview

1. Wood selection and cutting
2. Body routing for electronics
3. Neck carving and fret installation
4. Pickup installation
5. Wiring and soldering
6. Finishing and painting

> Electric guitar construction combines woodworking and electronics. Small differences in pickup placement, wiring, and body design can dramatically affect tone and playability. Construction also impacts future [[Electric/maintenance|maintenance]] requirements.
